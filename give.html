Where Your Generosity Goes

At Daybreak Community Church, our strength lies in active faith and community compassion. You can direct your tithes and gifts to any of the following specific funds, or support our general ministry.

Core Fund

Tithes & General Ministry

Faithfully sustains weekly Sunday 10:45 AM worship, pastoral care and preaching with Pastor Andrew Cole, discipleship classes, facilities at 1219 Main St, and daily church operations.

Local Outreach

Food Pantry & Student Meals

Directly supports our food collection partnership with LapelFoodPantry.com and funds our Wednesday night program preparing hot dinners for local kids and students during the school year.

Next Generation

Revolution Youth & Children

Empowers programs led by Youth Pastor Alex Small and Children’s Pastor Naomi Small, including kids & teen summer camp scholarships, Sunday school curriculum, and 30+ young adults in community sports leagues.

Global Missions

Overseas Missions & Relief

Provides vital humanitarian aid, medical supplies, and gospel resources to overseas missionaries and partner church plants reaching unreached communities across the globe.

Hope & Healing

Recovery & Sober Living Support

Supports Christ-centered recovery initiatives and sober living support, walking alongside individuals and families taking steps toward freedom and restoration.

Compassion & Care

Community Benevolence & Care

Provides emergency benevolence assistance, utility and heating relief, grocery vouchers, and compassionate pastoral care for local Lapel families walking through sudden hardship.

Three Simple Ways to Give

Whether you prefer giving securely online, by check in the mail, or dropping off physical canned goods for the food pantry, your contribution makes an eternal impact.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about giving, financial integrity, and tax deductibility at Daybreak.

Are my financial gifts tax-deductible?

Yes. Daybreak Community Church is a legally registered 501(c)(3) religious non-profit organization under federal law. All contributions are 100% tax-deductible. Annual giving statements are mailed to donors at the beginning of each calendar year.

Can I specify where my gift is allocated?

Absolutely. Through our online giving menu or check memo line, you can designate your contribution to Tithes & General Ministry, the Lapel Food Pantry & Student Meals, Revolution Youth & Kids, Overseas Missions, or Recovery Programs.

Do I need a PayPal account to give online?

No. PayPal processes our online donations securely, allowing you to pay with any major debit card, credit card, or bank card directly without having or creating a PayPal account.
