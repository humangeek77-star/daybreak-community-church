DAYBREAK MINISTRIES & OUTREACH

Putting our faith into action through student dinners, food relief, addiction recovery, youth sports, and global compassions.

Wednesday Night Classes & Student Hot Meals

Every Wednesday • 6:00 PM

Daybreak Community Church is in the business of changing and shaping lives. We host classes on Wednesday nights for kids, teens, ladies, and men starting at 6 p.m. Along with that, we serve free, nutritious hot meals to students during the school year. We realize that this may be the only hot meal of the day for some of our students.

Sports & Young Adult Fellowship

Year-Round Leagues & Fitness

Healthy bodies and healthy spirits go hand-in-hand! Our athletic leagues and outdoor activities connect church members and neighbors in a friendly, high-energy environment. From church softball to open-gym basketball and family park days, our sports ministry is one of the easiest ways to build lifelong friendships.

Lapel Community Food Pantry

Community Food Relief

No family in Lapel or Madison County should ever go to bed hungry. Partnering directly with Lapelfoodpantry.com, Daybreak provides non-perishable pantry essentials, fresh produce, and emergency relief to families experiencing difficult seasons or job transitions.

Anchored in Hope • Recovery & Renewal

Confidential Support & Freedom

We believe there is no pit so deep that God’s love and grace cannot reach. Our recovery initiative offers confidential, compassionate, Christ-centered support groups for individuals and families walking through substance use challenges, grief, hurts, and habits. You never have to walk the journey alone.

Missions & Global Compassion

Reaching the Nations

Daybreak actively invests in missionary families and humanitarian work across the globe — planting churches, funding medical clinics, building clean water wells, and providing disaster relief. We believe the Gospel is meant for all nations and every tribe.
