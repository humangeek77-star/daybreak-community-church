Our Heritage & Mission

ABOUT DAYBREAK COMMUNITY CHURCH

A Christ-centered family loving God, loving people, and making a lasting difference in Lapel, Indiana since 1998.

Our Roots & Journey

Our roots are growing deep and wide.

Daybreak Community Church knows that our strength lies not only in the words we stand by, but most importantly through the actions of our initiatives. Back in 1998 we realized that by working together we could overcome our challenges much more efficiently, and that is why we ultimately decided to launch Daybreak Community Church.

We are currently partnering with Lapelfoodpantry.com to reach neighbors in need across Madison County. Along with the food pantry, we serve free hot meals to students on Wednesday nights during the school year — recognizing that for some children, this is their only reliable hot meal. Join us as we reach our families!

“At Daybreak Community Church we are making a difference in the hearts and minds of those that we meet. We love God! We love people! We love YOU!”

Our Foundation

The Four Pillars of Daybreak

Everything we do centers around four life-changing steps designed to help every person flourish in Christ.

Pillar 01

Know God

Help people know God personally through vibrant Sunday worship, Scripture teaching, and an authentic relationship with Jesus Christ.

Pillar 02

Grow & Find Freedom

Grow alongside genuine friends through small groups, youth classes, and recovery communities that bring lasting life transformation.

Pillar 03

Discover Your Purpose

Discover the unique spiritual gifts, passions, and design God placed within you so you can live with meaning and fulfillment.

Pillar 04

Go Make a Difference

Go and put faith into action through student hot meals, food pantry distributions, sports ministries, and global outreach.
